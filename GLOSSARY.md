# Statement imports

## Language

**Funds blocked**: Money reserved by a payment mandate, such as an IPO application. A reservation is not a completed payment or investment purchase.

**Funds unblocked**: Release of money previously reserved by a mandate. A release is not money received or income.

**Card billing period**: The period covered by a card statement's billing cycle. The statement may list earlier transaction dates when those purchases enter this cycle; the original transaction dates remain authoritative.

**Account type**: The kind of money account: credit card, savings bank, current account, wallet or cash. Each type provides default compatible payment methods during setup.

**Payment method**: How an entry moved money, such as UPI or net banking. Both can use the same savings or current account; users do not need to define those links separately.

**Expense credit**: A separate reimbursement or refund that reduces Revenue Expense in its chosen category on the credit's own date. It is neither income nor recovery of an independently recorded loan.
