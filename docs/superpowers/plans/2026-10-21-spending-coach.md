# Private spending coach

**Goal:** Explain petty-expense and card-spending patterns with evidence and actionable review, extending the approved dashboard analytics.
**Architecture:** Pure local analysis over the existing ledger, statement metadata and cashback checks. Reuse dashboard filters and drill-down/edit sheets; add a compact coach panel with explicit observed/review/scenario labels. Reuse approved habit-suggestion controls with scoped source evidence. No external model calls, credentials, deployment or schema migration.

- [x] Test threshold boundaries, overlap, active/deleted/future filtering, missing income/baselines, card recognition, source/ledger separation, pending coverage, refund offsets and account/date scopes.
- [x] Add `SpendingCoach.tsx` with threshold/scenario controls, findings, card evidence review, guided questions and explicit habit approval. Keep the existing teal/slate design and warm warning accents; no generic chatbot or unsubstantiated claims of AI inference.
- [x] Wire the dashboard and scoped habit controls without changing existing import behavior. Verify mobile/desktop interactions, evidence/edit/filter/reload, empty states and card-review regression. Run typecheck/build and focused tests.

**Rules:** Petty means a user-selected upper payment amount (default ₹100), not waste. CC spending is posted personal expense recognized by CC payment mode, known card account or imported card-account evidence. Purchases/capex are not inferred from credits. Payment/refund/cashback source rows are separate from posted spending. Petty-on-card overlaps once in combined scenarios. Frequency and ticket-size contributions sum to the observed spending change, and are mathematical attribution rather than causal certainty. Baselines missing in recorded data are not zero-activity claims. Held amounts are not promised refunds; cashback variances require existing verified controls. Scenario savings are hypothetical; no ledger edits or habits without explicit user actions.
